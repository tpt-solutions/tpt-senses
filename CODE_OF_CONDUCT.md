# Code of Conduct

Everyone taking part in the `tpt-senses` project — in issues, pull requests, discussions and other
spaces — is expected to treat others with respect and good faith, regardless of background or
experience. Disagree with ideas, not people.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing others' private information
without consent, and other conduct a reasonable person would find hostile are not acceptable.

Project maintainers (TPT Solutions) may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other
contributions that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation for repeated or serious violations.

To report a concern, contact TPT Solutions through the repository's private reporting channel.
Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.

This document is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1
(<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>).
